About Lantern

Lantern is an unofficial, fan-made guide to the EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays (November 27 to December 30, 2026). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Disney.

Menu items, booth names and showtimes come from publicly available festival information and can change. Check in the park before you head over. The food and drink illustrations were made for Lantern.

Privacy: the short version

Lantern has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell or share your information with anyone.

What stays on your phone

Without signing in, everything you do in Lantern (stars, tried checks, your settings) stays on your phone.

Your location is used on your phone only, to show walking distances, routes and nearby alerts. It is never sent to us.

If you sign in to share with family

Signing in with Apple gives Lantern a private account ID and, the first time, your first name. Lantern doesn't ask Apple for your email address.

When you start or join a family group, we store your display name, the family name, your stars, tried checks, showtime fixes and any photos you add. Only people in your family group can see them. Photos are re-saved before upload, so they carry no location data.

Unless the person who started your family turns it off, your family's stars also count toward the anonymous festival-wide averages everyone sees. Those show only totals, never who rated what.

This information is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the United States, and is sent over encrypted connections.

Deleting your information

Leave a family: My Festival → Family group → Leave family. Your stars, checks and photos are removed from that family and stay on your phone.

Delete your account: My Festival → Family group → Delete account. This permanently deletes your photos, stars, tried checks and family membership from our servers and clears them from your phone. If you were the last person in a family, the family is deleted too.

To also disconnect Sign in with Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Lantern → Stop Using.

Children

Lantern isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.
